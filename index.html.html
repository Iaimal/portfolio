Research

Smart-grid load forecasting

Principal author — designed a hybrid TCN-BiLSTM architecture (three dilated causal TCN blocks feeding a bidirectional LSTM) that outperformed five recurrent and convolutional-recurrent baselines on two grids, with all 10 comparisons statistically significant (paired t-tests, p < 0.05).

Reached MAE 174.9 MW, R² = 0.9591 (AEMO NSW) and MAE 609.4 kW, R² = 0.9758 (GC-Z1) under a strict chronological 70/15/15 split, across single-step, 24-hour and 168-hour horizons. Tuned six hyperparameters with Optuna (TPE, 30 trials); an ablation confirmed the gain comes from combining convolutional and bidirectional-recurrent components; added 90% and 95% prediction intervals via MC-Dropout and split conformal prediction, with empirical coverage reported honestly (87–88% and 92–94%). Manuscript currently under review.
